Questions advisors actually ask

Before you apply.

Do I need my own RIA?

No. You can join as an investment adviser representative of an existing firm, or as the principal of your own RIA. What matters is that you are properly registered in the states where your members live. We check this during credentialing and we will tell you exactly which states you are cleared for.

If a member becomes my client, what does Boldin take?

Nothing. No percentage, no referral fee, no trailing cut, no time limit. You sign them directly under your own agreement and your own fee schedule. This is written into the advisor agreement, not a policy we can quietly change.

Can I sell products or earn commissions on these sessions?

Not on the platform. Boldin sessions are fee-only advice, paid by Boldin. You cannot recommend a product you are compensated on during a session, and every advisor signs an acknowledgment of that. What you do in your own practice, after a member has independently engaged you, is governed by your own firm and your own disclosures.

How much time does a session really take?

About 75 minutes all in: 15 of prep against the brief, 60 on video, and notes that most advisors finish inside 10 because the template is structured and the plan data is already there. Our modeling uses 1.25 hours per session, which is deliberately conservative.

What if a member is a bad fit, or hostile, or wants something I cannot give?

You can decline any booking before it starts and end any session in progress. You are paid in full either way, and you are never penalized in tier progression for a declined or ended session. Tell us what happened and we handle the member.

If a member asks for something outside the scope of planning advice, say so and refer out. That is the right answer and it is never held against your rating.

When and how do I get paid?

Every Friday by ACH, covering every session completed through the prior Friday. Five business days, consistently. You are paid whether or not the member books again, whether or not they liked the answer, and whether or not they act on your advice.

Can I pause, or take a month off?

Yes. Close your availability and nothing routes to you. Your tier and rating are held for 12 months of inactivity. There is no minimum after your first 10 sessions, and no penalty for seasonality, which most advisors need around tax season.

Is there any exclusivity?

None. Keep your firm, your book, your other platforms, your custodian relationships. Boldin is one line on your calendar, not a career decision.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing, at any stage. No application fee, no platform fee, no monthly software charge, no revenue share on your own clients. Boldin charges members for the session and pays you your rate out of it. That is the entire commercial relationship.

What happens if I do not get in?

We tell you why, in writing, and whether it is permanent. Most declines are fixable: a lapsed state registration, an expired E and O certificate, or not yet at three years of experience. You can reapply as soon as the reason is resolved.
